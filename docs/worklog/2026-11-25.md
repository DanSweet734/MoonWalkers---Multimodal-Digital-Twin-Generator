### Tool gaps found
1. **No hold for a mill:** `set_spec_frame` takes any `hold_drive`, and nothing estimates the
   sag (m g / k on a driven sliding joint holding weight) in `preview_twin` or `dry_run_twin`.
2. **Non-manifold points by hand:** the warning names the points, but finding a clean cut edge
   takes one preview per guess. A tool that tries a box face's position over a range, as my
   script did, or moves it off the mesh's vertices, would close it.
3. **Loose pieces are not shown:** `preview_twin` counts a cut part's pieces but neither lists
   nor draws them, so telling a shard from the machine needed my own script.
4. **The photo overlays' cameras:** they were fitted 7, 32 and 33 deg away from the corners'
   slots, so the overlays could not settle the table's place. A fit started at, or held near,
   the slot's azimuth would.
5. **Stand-in pieces:** that pieces may not meet end to end is reported only by `preview_twin`.
   And `repeat` steps in a line, so ten carousel pockets on a circle were ten pieces written by
   hand.
6. **Measuring the mesh:** `inspect_geometry`'s views carry no model-unit grid and there are no
   sections or box extents among the tools; the measurements came from my scripts (as in v4's
   report, section 8.3).
7. **The program's numbers come late:** `add_control` checks the program, but the reach, the
   touch-off and the duration show only in the dry run's `PROGRAM.md`.
