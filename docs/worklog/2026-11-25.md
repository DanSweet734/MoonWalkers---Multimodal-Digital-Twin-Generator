### Built
- **The setting** (`orchestrator/photos.py`): `CutoutEdit` (`op` add or remove, `points`, a
  `width` for a line, a `note`); `Cutout.edits`; the method `auto` (u2net, needs an edit). A
  cut-out without edits serialises as before, so old runs keep their reuse key.
- **The mask** (`generators/scripts/hunyuan3d_shape_job.py`): edits drawn in order with Pillow
  after the method's mask, before `min_piece`; a preview writes `masks/<view>.png`, outlines the
  edits on the sheet and, given masks to compare, prints each IoU.
- **Commands and tools:** `... pipelines.b cutouts <run> --compare <folder>`; `set_photo` takes
  edits; `preview_cutouts` says each photo's method, share kept, edits and IoU.
- **The API** (`api/jobs.py`): `PUT .../photos/{source}/cutout` (through `Workspace.set_photo`;
  409 once generated) and `POST .../cutouts` (the preview, with `MOONWALKERS_GEN_ROOT`; 503 and
  the command without it). A job's photos carry their cut-out, the preview's mask and whether it
  is `current` or `stale`; `cutouts_editable` on the job.
- **The dashboard:** "Edit the cut-out" beside each generator photo on a job's page
  (`components/cutout.tsx`, `lib/cutout.ts` with 13 tests): the method, the box by dragging,
  SAM's points, polygons and lines to add or remove, a width and a note, undo, save, and "Save
  and preview" with the kept part shown over the dimmed photo.
- **The warnings** (`pipelines/b/spec_warnings.py`), in `preview_twin`'s issues and
  `dry_run_twin`'s `spec_warnings` (printed by the `dry-run` command too).
