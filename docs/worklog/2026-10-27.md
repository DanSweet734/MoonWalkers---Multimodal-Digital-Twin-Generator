## What was done
1. **Reference facts** (`pipelines/b/grizzly_t33901.reference.json`): 36 facts from the manual,
   each quoting its PDF page, plus Daniel's directions as a `user_input` source.
   - New from the data sheet (p. 9): "T-Slot Size ... 9/16 in.", "Number of T-Slots ... 4",
     "Quill Flange/Collar Diameter ... 2-1/8 in.", used by the stand-ins.
   - **The quote checker** (`python -m moonwalkers.pipelines.b.quotes`). It finds each cited PDF
     under `data/` by its path or its SHA-256, and reads pypdf's text loosely: whitespace ignored
     ("T ilt", p. 32), words split at line ends joined ("prede-"/"termined", p. 7), "..." for dot
     leaders, fraction slashes and dashes as plain ones.
     - T33901: **42 of 42 quotes on their cited pages.**
     - HA5C: 15 of 16 checkable quotes found; 6 cannot be checked (a web page, Daniel's answers).
       The one miss is the drawing's stacked fraction "2 3/16", which pypdf reads as "2 -10 NS-2A",
       "3", "16" (research note of 2026-10-17). The file is unchanged.
     - First version's flaw, found by its own test: "Spindle Travel ... 4 in." matched "3-1/4 in.",
       since "..." could swallow part of a number. What "..." stands for now starts and ends
       between words.
2. **Measured run-001's mesh** (decision 0024; read-only scratch scripts, then the new
   `inspect --circles-along z`): the column (r 0.0789-0.0797 at every clear height), the rack's
   bump (+60 to +105 deg around the column), the base's work surface (z -0.892: 2.11 in above the
   floor, as Phase 1's inferred 2-1/8 in), the guard and the chuck's end about the spindle, the
   crank's hub (x -0.190, z -0.364), the arm (in line with the column, centred z -0.371) and the
   table's top (z -0.230).
3. **Read the photos for the stand-ins,** each against a cited size in the same photo (crops made
   with the generator environment's Pillow, read-only, `-I`, as in Phase 4; nothing installed):
   - `table_top.png` against the 11-1/4 in table: rim 3/4 in, gutter 1/2 in, plate 8-3/4 in,
     diagonal slots from 1.6 in out, 1 in hole, corners rounded 1-1/2 in;

   - Fig. 36 and `front_operator_right.png` against the 3-1/8 in column: the crank's hub, an arm

     and to the front"; the stand-in follows the photos;
   - Figs. 37 and 48: the rack about 3/4 in wide and 0.3 in thick, with fine teeth.
4. **Built** (decision 0027):
   - `parts_spec.py`: the several-part spec and its assembly;
   - `stand_ins.py`: `solids` and `slotted_table` (a signed distance meshed by surface nets);
   - `part_views.py`: the views and overlays moved from the spike, with stand-ins drawn through the

   - `pipeline.py`: the result for a several-part spec, a scale from any cited length, the spec's

   - `build.py`: a check file per behaviour, the views and the recorder in the run folder;
   - `inspection.py` and `cli.py`: `inspect --preview --spec <draft> [--no-photos]` and
     `inspect --circles-along {x,y,z} --at ...`; `mesh_tools.fit_circles_along`.
5. **The T33901's spec,** composed by a scratch script (inches to metres) from the Phase 4 cut list
   (`part_spike.T33901_CUTS`) and the Phase 1 joints (`primitive_drill_press.build_joints`):
   11 twin parts (4 stand-ins), 3 removed parts, 10 joints (3 fixed). Previewed and looked at:
   `parts.png`, each part, and both overlays. Two fixes after looking:
   - the table's slots cut through the rim's corners; in the photo they end at the plate. Each slot

6. **Dry run** on a scratch copy of the data folder (the run's request and mesh, the inputs, a fresh

7. **Built `grizzly/t33901@v1`** in run-001 (2 min 24 s): `twin.usdc`, six check files, `views/`,
   `record_joints.py`, `RUN.md`.
   - **Our validator:** 0 errors; 5 infos (the mimic coupling, the four locks).
   - **NVIDIA's:** 0 errors; 3 warnings in every file: non-manifold points in the chuck and the
     crank. My first fix for the co-located points (welding them) caused them. The weld is gone;
     `shape_mesh` now refuses pieces that share a point, and the spec's pieces overlap by 0.5 mm.
     Confirmed by a second dry run (below). v1 stands: run folders never change.
8. **Decisions 0024** (the scale is the overall height, 0.496536 m per model unit; masses stay
   placeholders) **and 0027** (joints and stand-ins in the spec), both proposed.

## Results
- Scale: 0.496536 m per model unit (39 in over 1.9950 units). Size checks, all under 10%: width
  +1.6%, depth -4.7%, column -0.8%, spindle to column -5.9%, base length +5.8%, base width +3.6%.
- Not checkable: spindle to base and to table (the nose is inside the solid guard).
- The photo fits are Phase 4's: IoU 0.796 for both fed photos, at -58.1 and +43.9 deg. The overlays
  put every part, stand-ins included, on its part of the photo, with Phase 4's misfit of a few
  percent, largest at the table.
- Tests: 637 passed without the GPU and slow tests (586 at Phase 4's end; 51 new). Ruff clean.

## What was done

  has one. To confirm with Daniel. The generated hub is the one decision 0023 logged, with one
  handle's root fused to the head's wall; from the front, that root sits over the depth stop's ring.

  0.046 m in front of the pinion's axis and 0.008 m below it, from the mesh, read-only.

  the quill down against its spring, an acceleration drive on the quill alone. In Phase 1 the three
  handles were balanced, and the quill went fully home. That makes decision 0024's "nothing in
  levels 1 and 2 depends on mass" wrong for a joint followed through a lever: corrected there.
