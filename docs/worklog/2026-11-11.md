## The lab's machines plan, Phase 4: the Haas TM-1, levels 1 and 2
**Goal:** build `haas/tm1` from the lab's corner photos: the frame, the axes' carriages, the
head, the spindle and the tool carousel, each axis sliding its cited travel, the spindle turning,
the carousel indexing its cited pockets (plan, Phase 4's checklist). Branch
`feat/lab-phase-4-tm1`, from `main` after PR #30.

### A slide check (tool gap): `haas/tm1@v2`
- **The gap:** the build's check files spin driven turning joints but nothing ran a driven
  sliding joint through its travel. The arm's ranges were measured from its spin recordings; a
  mill's axes had none.
- **The change** (`pipelines/b/build.py`): `checks/slide.usdc`.
  - Each driven sliding joint starts at its lower limit and slides to its upper one at its cited
    top speed (else 25 mm/s).
  - The velocity drive's damping is 10000: an acceleration drive's speed error under gravity is
    g / damping, about 1 mm/s; the spin check's 20 would let the head fall while rising.
  - One test.

  joint's column (`<joint> (m)`) is now scored in millimetres against the scorecard's 1 mm; the
