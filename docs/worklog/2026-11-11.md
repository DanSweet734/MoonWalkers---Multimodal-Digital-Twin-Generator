- **The joints:**
  - `y_axis`, frame -> saddle, ±6 in about the middle (the photographed Y is not measured);
  - `x_axis`, saddle -> table, -4.6 to +25.4 in: as photographed the table is 10.4 in to the

### A slide check (tool gap): `haas/tm1@v2`
- **The gap:** the build's check files spin driven turning joints but nothing ran a driven
  sliding joint through its travel. The arm's ranges were measured from its spin recordings; a
  mill's axes had none.
- **The change** (`pipelines/b/build.py`): `checks/slide.usdc`.
  - Each driven sliding joint starts at its lower limit and slides to its upper one at its cited
    top speed (else 25 mm/s).
  - The velocity drive's damping is 10000: an acceleration drive's speed error under gravity is
    g / damping, about 1 mm/s; the spin check's 20 would let the head fall while rising.
  - One test.
- **The scorer** (`pipelines/b/score.py`) read only turning joints' recordings. A sliding
  joint's column (`<joint> (m)`) is now scored in millimetres against the scorecard's 1 mm; the
  report's columns carry their unit. One test.
- **v1 cannot take the new file:** a run folder's outputs are never rewritten. So run
  `2026-11-11_run-002` is v1's request (its own job id, `new_version`), generator output, masks,
  reference facts and spec, copied by `part_cutting/2026-11-11_spec/copy_run.py`.
  - The first dry run reused v1, because v1's request says `new_version: false`. The copy now
    asks for a new version.
- **v2 dry-run clean, then built:** `haas/tm1@v2`, both validators clean on `twin.usdc` and
  the three check files.
- **`slide.usdc` on v2:**
  - `y_axis` -6 to +6 in;
  - `x_axis` -4.6 to +25.4 in;
  - `z_axis` -6 to +10 in;
  - all three at 84.7 mm/s.
- The runs' `reference_facts.json` were copied in after the builds; the scorer reads it from the
  run.
