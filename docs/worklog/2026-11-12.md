## A finding for the Rebel twin: the 2025 document gives other numbers
Pages 8 and 15 of the 2025 document were rendered with pypdfium2 and the backend's `write_png`,
and read by eye, to check the pairing of its table values.

| | 2022, MAT0075179.25 (what `main` cites) | 2025, MAT0075223.25 |
|---|---|---|
| Reach, 6 DOF | 664 mm (PDF p. 2) | 670 mm (PDF p. 8, printed p. 14) |
| Mass, 6 DOF | "< 8" kg (PDF p. 2) | 8,2 kg (PDF p. 8) |
| Max. payload, 6 DOF | 2 kg | 2,0 kg |
| Operating voltage | not on p. 2 | 24V (PDF p. 8) |
| L1 / X1 / X2 / X3 / X4 | 660 / 252 / 237 / 297 / 126 mm, "ReBeL-6DOF-00" (PDF p. 6) | 670,2 / 252,1 / 241,5 / 299,9 / 128,8 mm, "REBEL-6DOF" (PDF p. 15, printed p. 29) |
| X5 (overall height) | not given | 922,3 mm |

- The 2025 table names the type "REBEL-6DOF", with variants -00 (integrated control) and -OS
  (open source). It does not name -02.
- [INFERENCE] The newer edition may describe a newer arm, so which one fits the lab's
  REBEL-6DOF-02 is not confirmed. Under the evidence rules, both values are named here.
- Nothing in `main`'s `igus_rebel6dof02.reference.json` was changed. It is Daniel's, on `main`,
