## Phase 6: parts hidden in the checks, the VF-3's spec, and `haas/vf3@v1`
**Goal:** build `haas/vf3` (levels 1 and 2) from run `2026-11-16_run-001`, then stop for
Daniel's Isaac Sim check (the plan, Phase 6).

**The problem the build met.** The generator made the VF-3's enclosure one closed shell, and its
doors' windows are solid. The saddle, the table, the head and the trunnion all sit inside it, so
none of them shows in the twin, and a check could not show them move. The TM-1 was open
(Phase 4); the drill press had nothing inside.

**What was added (code):** a spec may list parts that are written invisible in every check file
and program folder, never in `twin.usdc` (`hidden_in_checks`; to be recorded in decision 0036,
with the trunnion's axes, before the PR).
- `usd/exporter.py`: `export_twin` and `build_stage` take `hidden_parts`. A hidden part's body is
  made invisible after it is written, so its mass, collision and joints stay. An unknown part
  raises `ExportError`.
- `pipelines/b/parts_spec.py`: `PartsSpec.hidden_in_checks`, validated against the cut and
  stand-in parts.
- `pipelines/b/build.py`: `hidden_in_checks(run_dir)`. A build with hidden parts writes a new
  check file, `checks/inside.usdc` (the twin with them hidden, holding, for typed targets). It
  passes them to every check file and to `write_program_folder`.
- `control/export.py`, `mill_export.py`, `arm_export.py` pass `hidden_parts` through to
  `build_stage`. `control/cli.py`'s `twin_hidden_parts` reads them from the stored twin's run.
- Tests: `test_usd_exporter.py::test_hidden_parts_are_invisible_and_keep_their_physics`;
  `test_pipeline_b_parts_spec.py`: a validation case, and
  `test_parts_hidden_in_checks_show_in_the_twin_and_hide_in_its_checks_and_programs`.

## Phase 6: A and B in the Haas reader (decision 0036), and the VF-3's two programs
**Goal:** level 3 for the VF-3. Phase 5's reader learns the trunnion's two axes, and runs a
program that tilts and turns the part (the plan, Phase 6). Daniel's word after his level-2 check:
"you're clear to get to work".

**Sources read** (the research note's update of this date): the 2012 Classic manual's pages on
A and B (PDF pp. 172-177, 181, 191, 225, 233, 244-245, 271, 277), text taken with pypdfium2.
- A and B are always in degrees.
- G00, G01 and G28 take them.
- G93 is needed for simultaneous 5-axis motion.
- G01's F is units per minute at Settings 34 and 79's diameter. One example (p. 173) says degrees
  per second.
- "Rapid moves are not allowed" sits under 4- and 5-axis cutting.
- The 2011 rotary manual's right hand rule (p. 34).
