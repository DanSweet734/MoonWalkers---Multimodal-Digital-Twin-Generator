### The orientation fault
`orient.py` (scratchpad) showed 19 of the 29 photos with EXIF orientation 6. The generator job
opened photos with `Image.open` alone, so it would have fed them sideways. The drill press's
photos were PNGs, so this never showed. Fixed: `_open_upright` (`ImageOps.exif_transpose`) in
`generators/scripts/hunyuan3d_shape_job.py`; the backend reads only the job's `prepared/` PNGs, so
nothing else changes. `docs/setup/generators.md` says so.
