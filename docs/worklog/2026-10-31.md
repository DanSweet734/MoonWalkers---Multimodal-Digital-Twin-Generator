## What was done
1. **Read the session log** `kit_20261031_000713.log`: 0 errors, Kit 110.4.0. Both scripts ran
   through `simulation_manager`, and every scenario step printed at its planned time. Five runs:
   - runs 1 and 2: `drilling-stroke`;
   - run 3: `drilling-stroke`, then live use;
   - run 4: `no-key-no-start`;
   - run 5: `live`.
2. **Measured the recordings** with `python -m moonwalkers.control compare`:
   - **As planned:** the switch, the spindle (95% in 0.95 s, 790.0 RPM, below 1% 4.95 s after the
     stop), the handles (57.08 rad/m) and the quill pressing on the stop at 43.00 mm.
   - **Not as planned: the quill swings about the hand.** It overshoots to the stop at 3.5 s
     during the approach (planned 12.1 s), and the swing's errors shrink from 12 mm, every
     1.33-1.48 s. Each release bounces 4-5 times, as v3-v6 did.
3. **Found the cause** (`reflected_inertia.py`, a scratch script run read-only with `-I` in the
   generator's environment, which has scipy 1.16.3 and trimesh 4.9.0; the backend has no scipy):
   - The stand-in hub's convex hull, 6.4 kg at the placeholder density, has 0.073 kg m2 about the
     handles' axis. Through the link that weighs on the quill as 237 kg, against the quill's own
     1.91 kg: M = 124.8.
   - The predicted period (2.87 s) and damping ratio (0.090) match the measured ones (2.8 s, about
     0.10).
4. **Checked where the spring is:** p. 40, "the spring housing is located on the left side of the
   headstock", on the pinion's axis on the mesh (decision 0027).
5. **Recorded the check** in the controls folder's `PROGRAM.md` and the run's `RUN.md`, and in
   decision 0025 ("Isaac Sim check"), with three options for the fix.

## Results
- Level 3's control logic runs in Isaac Sim as the manual describes. The live controls work in the
  Property panel, under "Raw USD Properties".
- The stroke's motion needs the quill's dynamics fixed: the swing comes from the stand-in hub's
  placeholder inertia, reflected through the link.
- The speed list was not tried in Isaac Sim.
