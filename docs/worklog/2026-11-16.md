### The trunnion's axes, from the 2012 manual (96-8000 Rev AP; PDF pages, printed = PDF - 12)
- **Haas's names:** the 4th axis is A, the 5th is B: "Jog and command the 5th axis using the B
  address" (PDF p. 177); "the 'A' and '-A' keys ... will select the A axis for jogging. The B axis
  can be selected for jogging by pressing the Shift button" (p. 173).
- **A tilts, B turns on A:** the figure "Axis motion on the VR-11 Mill and the Haas TRT 210" (p. 171)
  shows a cradle tilting about its long axis, "A-Axis ±120º", carrying a round platter,
  "B-Axis 360º"; "the distance between the plane of the B-axis platter and the A-axis centerline
  on tilting rotary products" (p. 177). The lab's trunnion has the same layout (`133959`): a cradle
  between two end housings, its tilt axis along the operator's left-right, a platter with a vise.
- **The mill's own axes:** "Setting 30 (and Setting 78 for the 5th axis) specifies a parameter set
  for a given rotary unit ... The rotary model highlighted in the control must match the model
  engraved on the rotary unit's identification plate" (p. 175); G00 and G01 take "A Optional A-axis
  motion command" (p. 181). This confirms the plan's inference: the G-code reader carries over,
  and decision 0026's HA5C controller does not.
- **Brakes:** "M10/M11 engages/disengages the A-axis brake / M12/M13 engages/disengages the B-axis
  brake" (p. 172). **Homing:** "All Haas indexers home in the clockwise direction as viewed from
  the front" (p. 176).
- **Two passages disagree on a rotary feed:** "G01 A90. F50. will rotate the A-Axis 90 degrees at
  50 degrees per second" (p. 173), against Setting 34: "The feedrate in a program is always inches
  per minute ... the control must know the diameter of the part being machined in the A-axis in
  order to compute angular feedrate" (p. 271). Also "Rapid moves are not allowed" (p. 173, in the
  five-axis notes) against G00's "A Optional A-axis motion command" (p. 181). For decision 0036.
- **Not in the manual, so stand-ins until a source:** the lab's trunnion's model (its plate is
  not readable in the photos; the visit list's item 10), so the TRT 210's ±120° and 360° are a
