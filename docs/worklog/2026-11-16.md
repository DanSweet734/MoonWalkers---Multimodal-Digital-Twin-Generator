## Phase 5: mill programs on the TM-1 (to Daniel's Isaac Sim check)
**Goal.** Phase 5 of `docs/lab-machines-plan.md`: Haas's G and M codes read into the axes' motion
at cited rapid and feed rates, run on `haas/tm1@v3`, measured by the tool tip's path (level 3).
Branch `feat/lab-phase-5-mill-programs`.

**Daniel's three questions first, from the filed manuals** (research note
`docs/research/2026-11-16-haas-mill-programs.md`, sections 1-3):
- **Directions:** a program's coordinates are the tool's against the table (2009 manual, PDF p.
  148); +X the tool to the operator's right (O40001's "top right corner", 2023 manual, p. 102;
  O40002's holes, pp. 106-107), +Y towards the back [INFERENCE], +Z up (2009, PDF p. 65). In the
  twin: Haas X = -`x_axis`, Y = +`y_axis`, Z = +`z_axis`, each from home.
- **Home:** machine zero, where a zero return goes (PDF p. 161). Z at the top (PDF pp. 53, 67);
  Y with the table towards the operator [INFERENCE] (2023 manual, pp. 102, 105); **X home a
  stand-in** (the + end; no source gives it). The twin's 0 is machine X -4.6, Y -6.0, Z -10.0 in.
- **The tool tip:** a point on the spindle's axis, a stand-in 3.0 in tool below the nose's face,
  its length offset touched off on the twin as the manual's Tool Offset Measure does (PDF p. 67)
  on a stand-in 2.0 in block of stock: H01 = -14.8053 in.

**What was done.**
- **Research note** (above): the G and M codes Haas's examples use, with pages; what is refused;
  the stand-ins (section 6). The 2009 manual's own examples need corner rounding and cutter
  compensation with an offset, so the 2023 manual's O40001 and O40006 are the programs.
- **Decision 0035 (proposed):** our own reader, no library (none asked for); a direction and a
  home end per axis in the program's machine map, the joints' signs worked out from their axes
  and the tool's or work's side; G00 per axis at the rapid, G01-G03 along the path at F, a
  stand-in 1000 mm/s^2, every block ending at rest; the plan starts at the twin's 0 with a zero
  return; the shared runner drives sliding joints.
- **Code** (`backend/src/moonwalkers/control/`):
  - `haas_gcode.py`: the reader (`GcodeProgram`), the binding (`bind_mill`) and the planner
    (`run_gcode_program`): moves, arcs by I/J or R, full circles, helices, G04, G20/G21, G28,
    G43/G49 with the touch-off, G53, G54-G59, G90/G91, M03-M09, M06 with the carousel, M30;
  - `mill_export.py`: the program folder (`program.usdc` with the twin's hold, the spindle on a
    velocity drive, the stand-in tool, stock and drawn path; `trajectory.json`, `program.nc`,
    `PROGRAM.md`); it measures the nose's face and the table's top on the stage;
  - `isaac_runner.py`: sliding joints ("linear" drive, metres), `moonwalkers:program:positions`,
    and speed-only joints (the spindle); the HA5C and arm folders run as before;
  - `compare.py`: `compare_mill`, by the tool tip from positions only (the hand-off's note on the
    velocity column);
  - the command line: `show` lists a Haas program's blocks; `run` takes `--acceleration` (mm/s^2)
    and `--pocket-time`;
  - the two packaged programs, `control/programs/haas-o40001-basic-program.json` and
    `haas-o40006-cutter-comp.json`, with their set-ups' stand-ins and reasons.
