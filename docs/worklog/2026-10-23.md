## Causes and fixes (all in run `2026-10-23_run-002`)
- **The spindle fell asleep** [INFERENCE].
  - The PhysX schema's `physxArticulation:sleepThreshold` defaults to 0.00005 ("Mass-normalized
    kinetic energy threshold below which the articulation may go to sleep").
  - The slow chuck's energy is about r²ω²/4 = 3.3e-5. At 340 RPM it is 0.15, and the PoC test
    spindle at 30°/s was 6.2e-5, which is why that one kept turning in Phase 2.
  - Fix: `keep_awake=True` (`physx_schemas.keep_awake`, decision 0017). It is off by default, so
    older twins do not change.
- **The rack and pinion jammed** [INFERENCE].
  - Its ratio's sign was wrong for our joint frames, so the handles hit their limit about 3° away
    and held the quill down.
  - Fix: the ratio now takes the mimic gearing's sign (decision 0019). The mimic stays the chosen
    coupling.
- **The free table's height** was held by a hold drive of stiffness 100, which sags 0.098 m under
  gravity. It is now held by equal limits, the lock that held (decision 0018).
- **The recorder** created its view with the default stage id (-1). It now uses
  `SimulationManager.get_physics_simulation_view()`, or creates a view with the stage's id and
  `set_subspace_roots("/")`, as `SimulationManager` itself does. Its array reading
  (`isaac_recorder.dof_row`) handles NumPy, warp and torch.
- **The quill alone barely moves under a push** [INFERENCE]. Through the coupling the handles'
  lever is about 10 times stronger (178 mm × 57.1 rad/m). That is realistic, and was not changed.
