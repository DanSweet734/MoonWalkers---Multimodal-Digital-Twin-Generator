### 3. The controller, tested outside Isaac Sim
- **`control/isaac_controls.py`** (copied into a folder as `run_controls.py`):
  - `Controller` holds the switch's rules, the speed table, the hand and the spindle's ramps;
  - `ScenarioPlayer` plays a scenario's steps one after another;
  - the Isaac Sim part reads and writes the `Controls` prim's attributes on every physics step.
- **`control/manual.py`:**
  - the specs' models: `SwitchSpec`, `SpeedSpec`, `OperatorSpec`;
  - `manual_controls`, which pairs a command set with the behaviours it names;
  - `controller_spec`, the runner's JSON, built with what the exporter writes for the quill:
    spring target, stiffness, preload, gravity offset;
  - `plan_scenario`, which plays a scenario at 60 Hz with the same controller;
  - `predict_quill`, the quill's reference response.
- **`control/controls_export.py`:** writes the controls folder. **`control/compare.py`:**
  measures a recording against a plan. **The CLI** gains `controls` and `compare`.
- **The exporter** writes a cited `max_velocity` as `physxJoint:maxJointVelocity` (deg/s or m/s,
  with `PhysxJointAPI`; `physx_schemas.set_max_joint_velocity`). This was deferred since decision
  0010. The Phase 2 test twin has no cited speed, so its fixture tests are unchanged.
- **The joint recorder** writes velocities, and names a recording after the scenario that played.
- **The build** writes `programs/<command set>/` beside the program folders and lists it in
  `RUN.md`. **The agent's `add_control`** checks the three specs.
- **The T33901's reference facts:**
  - the three behaviours;
  - two sources, the UofT module and the UF handout;
  - three issues for the stand-ins.

  The quote checker found all 59 quotes on their pages.

**Found while planning, and fixed:**
- **The quill sags under gravity.** The spring drive is an acceleration drive, so gravity holds the
  quill g/k = 9.81 / 600 = 16.4 mm past the drive's target, as decision 0018 measured on a softer
  drive. With the hand's target on the hand, the planned quill ran 16 mm ahead and reached the 43 mm
  stop during the approach. The hand now aims 16.4 mm short while it holds the quill. A first try
  had the sign the wrong way round, caught by the plan: the quill ran 33 mm ahead.
- **Moving the paddle down** printed "the motor does not start"; now that line comes only when the
  paddle goes up.
- **Every input change now says what it did** ("power connected"), so a scenario's timeline and
  the live console show each step.

**Tests:** 38 new, in `test_control_manual.py` (24), `test_control_controls_export.py` (8),
`test_control_compare.py` (4), a build test in `test_pipeline_b_parts_spec.py` and an
`add_control` test in `test_mcp_server.py`. Quick suite: 685 passed, 5 deselected (3 min 31 s).
`ruff check` and `ruff format --check` clean.
