## Limits found
- **Parts tables lose their rows.** In the parts list, the REF, PART # and DESCRIPTION columns
  come out as separate runs of lines, so a reference number is not tied to its part number. The
  docstring says tables are not rebuilt.
- **Part numbers in tables are not candidates.** 159 lines of the parts list hold a `PT33901...`
  number; none is a candidate, because the pattern needs a label ("Part No", "Model", "SKU").
- **The label pattern accepts ordinary words.** "this model is quiet" gives the candidate `is`,
  and "Model: T33901." keeps the full stop. None appeared in the three Grizzly PDFs.
- **Exploded views are not extracted.** They are vector art (drill-press plan), and the parser
  exports embedded images only.
- `ruff check` on `catalog_parser.py`: 4 errors (I001, two BLE001, E501), and it needs
  reformatting. Not fixed: left for Sarkis.
- The parser is not wired into `CatalogPipeline`, which still answers a `pdf_catalog` source
  with "PDF catalog extraction is not implemented."

## Next step
- Sarkis decides whether to tighten the label pattern and how the parser's output becomes cited
  facts in a `PipelineResult` (a decision record, since it changes what Pipeline D accepts).
