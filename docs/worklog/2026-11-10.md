## Phase 3 of the lab's plan: the ReBeL arm's program (level 3), up to Daniel's check

**Goal:** the plan's Phase 3 checklist: research iRC's programs and CRI, run a program of joint
moves at no more than the cited 45°/s on `igus/rebel6dof02@v3`, and get it ready for Daniel's
Isaac Sim check. Branch `feat/lab-phase-3-rebel-arm-control`.

**Research** ([note](../research/2026-11-10-irc-programs-and-cri.md)). Searches found igus's iRC
command reference (06/2020) and Commonplace Robotics' iRC user guide (V14.5, 2025), both fetched
with curl; RobotShop's and Mouser's guides answered HTML instead of a PDF. The CPR wiki's page on
transferring programs gives a program's XML; Commonplace's CRI library (MIT) and iRC_ROS give
CRI's messages and iRC's angle offsets; RoboDK's CPR post-processor (Apache-2.0) gives Wait and
Output tags, second-hand. All filed in `data/machines/igus-rebel6dof02/inputs/documents/irc/`,
with SHA-256 in `SOURCES.md`. Found:
- programs are XML; a Joint move gives `a1`-`a6` in degrees, `velPercent` ("Velocity in percent
  of the max joint velocities") and `acc` (percent of a maximum acceleration);
- how several joints share one move is not stated; "The joint velocities during the motion are
  constant" and "The robot moves as fast as the slowest joint allows" point to all joints starting
  and stopping together [INFERENCE];
- no source gives the ReBeL's acceleration. iRC_ROS's MoveIt limit (5.240 rad/s², 300.2°/s²) is
  used as a labelled stand-in;
- linear moves need inverse kinematics in a convention not given: left out, as the plan allows;
- iRC_ROS's driver adds its joint offsets both when it sends and when it reads, so its sign is
  inconsistent; decision 0031's arithmetic on the limits settles that iRC's angles are igus's.

**Built** (decision [0033](../decisions/0033-an-arms-joint-programs.md), proposed):
- `control/irc.py`: reads iRC's XML (Joint; Wait, Output; the wiki's endless Loop), refuses the
  rest with the reason, finds each axis's joint and its angle in the photographed pose from the
  twin itself (igus's cited range minus the twin's limits, both ends agreeing, rounded to 0.01°),
  and plans synchronised trapezoid moves from the twin's 0. A target outside the twin's range is
  refused, named in iRC's and the twin's degrees.
- `control/arm_export.py`: the program folder, with `program.xml` and `record_joints.py`; every
  joint carries its plan for the unchanged runner.
- **The hand-off's two gaps closed:** program and controls folders now take the twin's
  `hold_drive` and `self_collision` (from the build, and from a stored twin's run spec on the
  command line). The HA5C's and the T33901's specs give neither, so their folders are unchanged.
- `control compare` measures an arm: each joint against the plan, where it ended, its top speed,
  and when each move's joints all arrived within 0.5°.
- `add_control` (MCP) checks an iRC program; the agent procedure and the tool guide say how.
- The test program `rebel-test-joint-moves` (labelled: Claude Code's, not the lab's): each joint
  alone out and back at Vel 100 %, then all six at 50 % and home, Acc 40 %; 16 moves, 24.36 s.
