# Phase 4: a spike on cutting the T33901 mesh into parts

## Goal
The drill-press plan's Phase 4, in one session: compare ways to cut one mesh into several moving
parts on two test meshes, the box-and-cylinder drill press (labelled face by face) and the
T33901's generated mesh (judged against the manual's figures). Measure the share of faces in the
right part, seams, time, installs and licences, and whether a cut can be cited and reviewed.
Write decision 0023, and stop for review.

## What was done
- **Read the hand-off:** CLAUDE.md, the plan's Phase 4, decisions 0012 and 0022, the research
  note of 2026-10-24 (section 4), and the run's `MESH.md`.
- **Method (c), a learned segmenter, needs an install,** so it was asked first, with two
  candidates' licences and sizes (P3-SAM and PartField). Daniel chose **"Research only"**. Nothing
  was installed this phase.
- **The test meshes** (`pipelines/b/synthetic_parts.py`): the Phase 1 drill press as one mesh,
  three ways:
  - separate, as the plan describes it;
  - fused into one surface on a 4 mm grid (naive surface nets), since a generator fuses touching
    parts;
  - rounded, since the fused mesh's sharp creases flatter method (b).

  Every face is labelled with its part, and scored by area.
- **The methods:**
  - (a) ordered cut lists of cylinders, boxes and half-spaces (`part_cuts.py`), with seam
    measures;
  - (b) separate pieces and sharp-edge regions (`mesh_segments.py`), using a numpy union-find
    (`mesh_tools.face_groups`), since the backend has no scipy or networkx;
  - (d) marks carried from photos onto the mesh, and a camera fitted to a real photo by its
    silhouette (`photo_marks.py`), with a small PNG reader (`mesh_tools.read_png`), since the
    backend has no image library;
  - a runner for all of it (`part_spike.py`): report, numbers, coloured views, and the cut parts
    drawn over the fed photos.
- **The T33901's cut list,** measured on the squared mesh:
  - circle fits across z found the column (radius 0.079 units: the cited 3-1/8 in gives 0.501 m
    per unit, within 1% of the cited height's scale) and the chuck guard;
  - zoomed slices found the depth stop's rod;
  - lines fitted to the three handles found the hub;
  - the manual's Figs. 3, 4, 35, 46 and 48 settled what moves with what. The chuck guard and the
    depth stop's rod go down with the quill.

  It took five passes, each after looking at the coloured views. The passes are listed in
  `part_spike.T33901_PASSES`.
- **Tests** for every new module, with the slow ones marked: the fused mesh closed at 4 mm, and
  the runner end to end.

## Results
Full numbers in `docs/research/2026-10-24-part-cutting-spike.md`.
- **On the rounded synthetic mesh** (area in the right part):
  - (a) **0.996**, a ceiling, since its cuts come from the model's own sizes;
  - (b) 0.733 as pieces, 0.923 at 30° even with each region named after its main part;
  - (d) with perfect marks, **0.947** from the two front corners the T33901 has and 0.976 from
    four corners. The quill under the head and the arm under the table are never seen from
    raised front corners.
- **On the T33901:**
  - (a) gives 8 parts. Each moving part is one piece, or the mesh explains why not: the up
    handle's root is fused to the head's wall;
  - (b): the mesh is one piece, and its sharp-edge regions cross the parts;
  - (d): the two fed photos were placed on the mesh with a silhouette IoU of 0.796 each
    (azimuth -58.1° and +43.9°, against -49.1° and +40.9° from the generator's slots). The cut
    parts drawn over them land on the right parts of the photos, a few percent off by eye.
- **Found in the mesh, logged for Phase 5:**
  - the clear chuck guard is solid, hiding the chuck;
  - the column rack is a bump of at most 2.5 mm;
  - the depth stop's nut and bracket are one ring;
  - a handle is fused to the wall;
  - the crank points into the guessed back;
  - spindle to column is 5-6% short of the cited 7 in;
  - the spindle is 1.4 cm off the column's plane.
- **Fixes on the way:**
  - "stray faces" first counted every face outside a part's largest piece, which counted the
    frame's upper half, rightly cut off by the bracket. It now counts pieces under 2% of their
    part;
  - two oranges in the colour palette were too alike;
  - the camera fit refines by whole degrees, three times faster;
  - the fused mesh does not close at a 12 mm grid, so its closure test runs at 4 mm, marked slow.
- **Proposed:** decision 0023. (a) cuts the parts, in a cut list in the geometry spec; (d)'s
  camera fit draws them over the photos for review; (b) is not used to cut; (c) and (d)'s marks
  wait.
