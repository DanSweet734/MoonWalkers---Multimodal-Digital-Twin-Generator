## The black gaps (measured)
Measured with a short trimesh and numpy script on `meshes/body.glb` and `meshes/spindle.glb` of
run-003, in the twin frame:
- **The shared cut edge.** Both parts share 142 open-edge vertices within 60 mm of the axis.
  - Outer ring, on the nose's surface: 74 vertices, 30.4 to 32.7 mm from the axis, all within
    0.6 mm of the cut plane.
  - Inner ring, in the bore: 68 vertices, 13.7 to 16.6 mm from the axis, between 5.6 mm behind and
    5.1 mm in front of the cut plane.
- **What turning does.** The spindle's outer cut edge was turned about the joint axis, and for each
  vertex the distance to the nearest body cut-edge vertex was measured:

  | Turn | Median | Max |
  |---|---|---|
  | 0 deg | 0 mm | 0 mm |
  | 30 deg | 1.08 mm | 2.55 mm |
  | 90 deg | 1.09 mm | 2.83 mm |

- **Reading [INFERENCE].** At 0 degrees the two edges meet exactly. The generated nose is slightly
  out of round, so once it turns its edge no longer meets the body's. Through the slivers you see
  the parts' hollow insides, since each part is an open shell at the cut, and they render black.
  The gaps therefore show that the joint turned, and they are cosmetic: Daniel saw no collision or
  drift.
- **Fixes, later.** These need a decision when the seam matters (for example for a close-up demo):
  - cut with a true cylinder and plane and cap the cut faces;
  - or make the seam round.

  This is recorded in decision 0012's Isaac Sim section.

## Quarter-turn timing
The two quarter turns used the same drive (acceleration type, stiffness 100, damping 20, target
90 degrees):
- Phase 2 test twin, Oct 17: about two seconds.
- HA5C, Oct 18: under a second.

Both are estimates by eye. With an acceleration drive the response should not depend on the parts'
masses [INFERENCE]. So the difference may come from judging by eye, or from the Phase 2 file having
no time range, since it was written before the timeline fix. Neither is confirmed. The open question
in `docs/research/2026-10-17-usd-physics-for-isaac-sim.md` now holds both observations. Phase 6
should measure settle time in a scripted run.
