### Choices and why
- **Orientation.** Up `+y`, front `+z`, yaw 49.1°: at yaw 0 the base footprint's long axis was at
  49.1°. The base is cited as 18 in long by 10-1/2 in wide, so that axis runs front to back. Squared,
  the chuck and table point to +x and the handles are on +y, the operator's right (Fig. 3).
- **Origin.** The spindle's axis, (0.278, -0.021), from round fits of the guard (r 0.112, rms below
  0.0012) and of the generated chuck's tip.
- **Scale.** The overall height, 39 in over 1.9950 model units: 0.49654 m per unit (decision 0024).
- **17 parts and 16 joints, as decision 0027 lists them for v5.**
  - The parts: 6 cut, 7 removed, 11 stand-ins.
  - Every number in the cuts, the stand-ins and the joints was measured anew on this mesh, by
    `fit_circles` and by read-only scratch scripts. The scripts used the pipeline's own
    `load_mesh` and `orientation_matrix`, for slice maps, box bounds, local circle fits, and the
    column's radius by angle.
- **The depth stop: 43 mm, decision 0025's accepted value.**
  - The agent read Figs. 4 and 35 itself. Scaled by the nut's height they give 41.6 and 44 mm, which
    agree.
  - Scaled by the rod taken as 12 mm, they give 45-49 mm. The rod looks about 9 mm in Fig. 46, so
    that scale is the weaker one. Logged.
- **Handle splay: 28°, not decision 0027's 20°.**
  - The handles sit at 60, 180 and 300° (one level to the back, as every photo shows).
  - Fig. 3's camera is about 38° from the +y side. With these angles, Fig. 3's down handle projects
    vertical only at about 28°.
  - With 20°, the overall width was -12.6%, a warning; with 28° it is -5.7%.
- **Table rim 10.5 mm, not 10 mm.** At exactly 10 mm, the rim's and the plate's edges fall on the
  2 mm meshing grid. `surface_nets` then makes 40 zero-area faces, and the exporter refuses them
  (dry run 1).
- **Cut boundaries moved to clear NVIDIA's ManifoldChecker warnings** (dry run 2: 1 point in the
  frame, 1 in the quill, 2 in the arm).
  - The guard/quill boundary went from z 0.292 to 0.297, the quill's radius from 0.122 to 0.125,
    and the arm's back from x -0.010 to -0.020.
  - The values were found offline (`try_spec.py` in the scratchpad: the pipeline's
    `assemble_parts` plus a fan count per vertex) before going through the tools.
- **The quill's post-release wait is 3 s, not 5 s.** v8 was home 0.28 s after release (decision
  0025, "Re-check of v8").

### Gaps found in the tools (none fixed in this phase)
1. **No tool changes a photo's view** in a dashboard job, and an upload can tag a view wrongly. Here
   `request.json` was edited by hand on Daniel's word.
2. **Issues are append-only.** A wrong issue of the agent's own had to be corrected by hand in
   `reference_facts.json` before submitting.
3. **`add_reference_source(kind="file")` accepts only the data folder,** so a decision record is
   cited by URL.
4. **`fit_circles` fits the whole slice.** Along y or x that takes in the whole machine. A window
   (u, v ranges) would make it usable there; the agent wrote its own.
5. **`preview_twin` does not report zero-area faces or non-manifold points.** Only the dry run's
   exporter and NVIDIA's validator found them. Adding both checks to the preview would save two
   dry runs.
6. **Patch positions go stale when a cut moves.** The refusal names the nearest loop, which helps.
   Clearing a part's patches and previewing again was the quick way.
